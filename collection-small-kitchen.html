8 small-kitchen finds worth a closer look.

Less digging, fewer mystery lids, and more room to cook. Here are eight real products that address common small-kitchen frustrations—with the tradeoffs worth checking before you spend.

Choose the problem before the product.

Measure the drawer or cabinet first, decide which frustration matters most, and buy one practical upgrade—not an entire matching system. If your current setup already works, you can skip the purchase.
