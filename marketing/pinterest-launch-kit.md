## Design direction
- Vertical format 1000 x 1500 px (2:3 aspect ratio) for each original static pin.
- Cedar green, soft cream, warm neutrals, legible high-contrast text.
- Prominently label 'DIGITAL DOWNLOAD' on product-focused designs.
- Use actual product screenshots only with permission and when they faithfully represent the workbook; avoid fake screenshots.
- Create at least two distinct designs per landing page and avoid reposting identical creative repeatedly.
- Add alt text that describes the image and what the planner does; do not keyword-stuff.

## First two weeks
Week 1: Complete Pinterest business profile, verify domain, create five boards, publish Pins 1–5 on distinct days. Check all links.
Week 2: Publish Pins 6–10 on distinct days. Review outbound clicks in Pinterest analytics and Etsy listing visits; compare website referral traffic in Google Analytics.
No paid advertising or automation subscriptions required.

## Safety checks before publishing
- Confirm exact product capabilities, screenshots, prices, and Etsy listing URLs.
- Avoid promising sales, savings, SEO ranking, or wedding outcomes.
- Don't share files purchased from other creators or copyrighted visuals.
- Follow Pinterest's current spam, merchant and affiliate guidelines.
- All times and descriptions are a suggested plan, not scheduled or published content.
